# Video KYC — Product Overview

Verify any customer's **identity** over a live video call — in the browser, in minutes.

## Why it matters

Onboarding and compliance shouldn't mean branch visits, paperwork, or clunky apps. Video KYC lets a trained **Agent** confirm a **Client's identity** on a face-to-face video call: check their ID, match it to their live face, confirm they're a real person, and walk away with an audit-ready report. No downloads, works on any phone or laptop, and it's fully white-labeled as your own product.

## What you get

- **Live agent-assisted verification** — a real person guides the customer, so drop-offs and fraud both go down.
- **Identity checks built in** — automatic ID document reading, face-match against the ID, liveness (real person, not a photo/video), and sensitive-number masking (e.g. Aadhaar).
- **Audit-ready by default** — sessions can be recorded, and every verification produces a signed PDF report.
- **Zero friction to join** — one secure link per party, no login, no install.
- **Resilient** — if someone's connection drops, one tap rejoins the *same* session.

## How a session works

1. **Create** — generate a session; get an **Agent Link** and a **Client Link**.
2. **Share** — send the Client Link to the customer; the Agent opens theirs.
3. **Get ready** — a quick pre-call check confirms camera, mic, speaker, location, and connection quality.
4. **Connect** — both join a live video call.
5. **Verify identity** — the Agent captures the customer's ID (front/back) and a live selfie; the system reads the document, matches the face to the ID, and confirms liveness. Sensitive numbers are auto-masked.
6. **Complete** — the session ends with a verification report; the customer sees a Thank You screen.
7. **Rejoin** — if either side drops, **REJOIN** returns them to the same session instantly.

## Stakeholders

Banks, fintechs, insurers, marketplaces, and any business that must confirm *who someone really is* before onboarding them — remote KYC, identity verification, and document verification.
